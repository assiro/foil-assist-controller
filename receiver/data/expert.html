Throttle response curve

Applied on the receiver before sending the throttle to the VESC. With gamma < 1 you get more power for small trigger travel (easier take-off); with gamma > 1 you get finer control at the top end. Mix blends the exponential curve with the linear one (0% = linear, 100% = pure exponential). Default gamma 1.00 and mix 0% = linear, identical to before.

Battery read offset

Offset set in test.html: ... V

The VESC reads the battery voltage wrong. The board always uses VESC reading + offset, and the low battery alarm threshold is checked on that corrected value. So with a positive offset the real pack voltage at which the alarm fires is the threshold minus the offset (example: 1 V of offset with a 37.2 V threshold means the alarm fires when the pack is really at 36.2 V).
